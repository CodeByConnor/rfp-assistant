# Meridian — Corporate Profile

*Doc type: public | Last updated: 2026-06-30*

## Legal entity

- Legal name: **Meridian Data, Inc.**
- Incorporated in Delaware, United States.
- Headquarters: Seattle, Washington.
- No parent or holding company. Meridian Data, Inc. is the contracting
  entity.

## Company history

- Founded 2018.
- The Meridian platform reached general availability in 2020.
- Headcount 412: engineering 168, customer support 61, professional services
  54, sales and marketing 92, general and administrative 37.

## Ownership and funding

- Venture-backed, privately held.
- Most recent round: Series C, 85 million USD, closed September 2025.
- **Not profitable.** Cash runway at current burn is approximately 31 months
  as of the most recent board reporting.
- No merger, acquisition, or change-of-control event in the last 36 months,
  as acquirer or target.
- No material litigation, regulatory enforcement action, or data protection
  authority investigation in the last 60 months.

## Customer base

- 340 customers in production.
- 96 are in retail or grocery. 31 operate more than 100 physical store
  locations.
- Gross revenue retention 91 percent; net revenue retention 114 percent for
  fiscal year 2025.
- No customer represents more than 10 percent of total company revenue. The
  largest single customer represents 4.1 percent.

## References

Three reference customers of comparable profile are available to shortlisted
vendors on request, coordinated through the account team. Meridian does not
release reference contact details in a written RFP response.

## Corporate responsibility

Meridian has **no published environmental sustainability programme, carbon
footprint commitment, emissions reporting, modern slavery statement, or
supplier diversity programme**. No approved company position exists on these
topics. Route any such requirement to Legal and the Chief of Staff before
responding — do not draft a position in an RFP response.
