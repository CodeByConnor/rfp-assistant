# Meridian — Past RFP Answer Library

*Doc type: public | Last updated: 2026-02-10*

Reusable, pre-approved answers from past deals. Prefer citing the primary
source document (whitepaper, architecture, product overview) where one covers
the question; use this file for phrasing and tone precedent, and for the few
topics only recorded here.

## Q: "Describe your approach to sub-processors and third-party data sharing."

A: Meridian publishes a current sub-processor list at our trust centre,
covering cloud infrastructure, email delivery, monitoring, and support
tooling. We do not sell customer data. Every sub-processor engagement is
governed by a data processing agreement consistent with GDPR Article 28.
Customers receive 30 days' notice of a new sub-processor and may object; an
unresolved objection carries a right to terminate without penalty.

## Q: "Do you support multi-factor authentication for admin console access?"

A: Yes. TOTP-based MFA is available on all accounts and can be enforced
organisation-wide by an administrator.

## Q: "Will you complete our own security questionnaire rather than supplying
your standard documentation?"

A: Yes, for deals above $100k ACV. Below that threshold we supply our SOC 2
report, this answer library, and the security whitepaper. Turnaround for a
custom questionnaire is 10 business days.
