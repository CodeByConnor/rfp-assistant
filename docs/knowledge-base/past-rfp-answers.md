# Meridian — Past RFP Answer Library

*Doc type: public | Last updated: 2026-02-10*

Reusable, pre-approved answers to common RFP questions, drawn from past deals.
Answering agents should prefer citing the primary source doc (whitepaper,
overview) over this file when both are available, and use this file mainly
for phrasing/tone precedent.

## Q: "Describe your approach to sub-processors and third-party data sharing."

A: Meridian maintains a public list of sub-processors (cloud infrastructure,
email delivery, and monitoring vendors) at our trust center. We do not sell
customer data to third parties, and any sub-processor engagement is governed
by a data processing agreement consistent with GDPR Article 28 requirements.
Customers are notified of new sub-processors with 30 days' notice.

## Q: "What is your approach to environmental sustainability / data center
carbon footprint?"

A: Not previously answered in a past RFP. No approved company position
exists on this topic as of this writing — route to Legal/Sustainability

## Q: "Do you support multi-factor authentication for admin console access?"

A: Yes. MFA (TOTP-based authenticator apps) is available for all user
accounts and can be enforced organization-wide by an Admin.
