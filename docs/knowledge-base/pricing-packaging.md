## Subscription tiers (list price, annual contract)

| Tier | Monthly tracked profiles | List price / month |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | up to 250,000 | $2,500 |
| Growth | up to 1,000,000 | $7,500 |
| Enterprise | 1,000,000+ | Custom quote |

Alderwood at 2.1M profiles falls into Enterprise. Indicative Enterprise
pricing at that volume is $14,000–$19,000 per month depending on term and
included services. **Quote a range only after Deal Desk review.**

## Overage

- Billed monthly at **$0.005 per profile** above the contracted tier.
- Overage is charged on profiles above tier, not on total profiles.

## Uplift and term

- Standard annual uplift at renewal: **7%**. Cappable at 4% for a three-year
  commitment with Deal Desk approval.
- Three-year commitment discount: 12–18% off annual list.
- Standard payment terms: annual, invoiced in advance. Net 30. Quarterly
  invoicing available above $200k ACV with Finance approval.

## Implementation and services

- Implementation fee for a multi-source retail deployment: $45,000–$75,000.
- Professional services rates: Solutions Architect $285/hr, Data Engineer
  $240/hr, Project Manager $195/hr.
- Non-production sandbox: included on Enterprise, $900/month on Growth.

## Discount authority

- Account Executives: up to 15% off list.
- Enterprise deals above $150k ACV require Deal Desk approval.
- **Never disclose list tier pricing directly in a written RFP response.**
  Provide a custom quote instead.
