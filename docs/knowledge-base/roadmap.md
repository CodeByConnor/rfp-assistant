# Meridian — Public Roadmap (Directional, No Committed Dates)

*Doc type: public | Last updated: 2026-06-10*

> Forward-looking statement: roadmap items are directional and not a
> commitment to deliver any feature by any date. Do not promise roadmap
> items as committed deliverables in contracts or RFP responses without
> Product sign-off.

## In progress

- **ISO 27001 certification** — audit in progress, targeting Q1 2027.
- **SCIM provisioning GA** — currently in beta, targeting GA by end of 2026.

## Planned (not yet started or early design)

- **Managed Kafka streaming connector** — targeted for design in Q3 2026,
  no committed ship date.
- **Additional data residency regions** (Canada, APAC) — under evaluation,
  no committed timeline. US and EU remain the only supported regions today.
- **Single-tenant / dedicated infrastructure option** — has been requested
  by several Enterprise prospects; not currently on the committed roadmap.
