# Meridian — Public Roadmap (Directional, No Committed Dates)

*Doc type: public | Last updated: 2026-06-10*

> Forward-looking statement: roadmap items are directional and are not a
> commitment to deliver any feature by any date. Do not present a roadmap item
> as a committed deliverable in a contract or an RFP response without Product
> sign-off.

## In progress (committed, dated)

- **ISO 27001 certification** — audit in progress, targeting Q1 2027.
- **SCIM provisioning general availability** — currently beta on Enterprise,
  targeting GA by end of 2026.

## Planned (targeted, no ship commitment)

- **Managed Kafka streaming connector** — targeted for design in Q3 2026. No
  committed ship date.
- **Customer-managed encryption keys (BYOK)** — targeted for design in Q4
  2026. No committed ship date.

## Under evaluation (no timeline)

- **Additional data residency regions**, including Canada and APAC. Under
  evaluation only. US and EU remain the only supported regions. No timeline.
- **Automatic cross-region failover.** Under evaluation. Cross-region
  recovery remains a manual procedure today.

## Requested but not planned

- **Single-tenant / dedicated infrastructure deployment** — requested by
  several Enterprise prospects. **Not on the roadmap.** There is no design
  work, no target, and no intention to deliver this. Answer this as a
  capability the platform does not have, not as a roadmap item.
- **Native journey orchestration** — Meridian's product direction is to
  activate into a customer's existing execution tool rather than to build
  campaign orchestration. Not planned.
- **Marketing attribution modelling** — not planned.
